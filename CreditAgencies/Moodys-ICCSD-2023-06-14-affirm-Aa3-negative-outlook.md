## The action

**[verbatim]** New York, June 14, 2023 — Moody's Investors Service has affirmed Iowa City Community School
District, IA's Aa3 issuer rating and the Aa3 rating assigned to general obligation unlimited tax (GOULT)
bonds. Concurrently, we have assigned a negative outlook. At the close of fiscal 2023, the district had about
**$244 million in outstanding debt** including general obligation debt, sales tax debt and unamortized net
premium.

## Ratings rationale

**[partial]** The Aa3 rating reflects the district's diverse economic base, **growing enrollment**, and resident
income and wealth. The district's **financial reserves are thin**, partially mitigated by a high degree of
revenue-raising flexibility from its cash reserve levy. The rationale references the stabilizing institutional
presence of the University of Iowa and the University of Iowa Hospitals & Clinics.

**[verbatim]** The Aa3 rating on GOULT debt is rated at the same level as the Aa3 issuer rating given the
district's full faith and credit pledge with authority to raise ad valorem property taxes unlimited as to rate
or amount.

## Rating outlook — the key passage

**[verbatim]** The negative outlook reflects the district's trend of **declining fund balance and cash**. If
reserves do not begin to meaningfully improve, the rating will likely move downward.

## Factors that could lead to an upgrade

**[verbatim]** Very large increase in fund balance and cash

## Factors that could lead to a downgrade

**[verbatim]**
- Absence of material improvement to fund balance and cash
- Substantial increase in leverage and/or fixed costs

## Legal security

**[verbatim]** The district's outstanding rated bonds are backed by the district's general obligation unlimited
property tax pledge of all taxable property within the district without limitation as to rate or amount.

## Profile

**[partial]** Iowa City CSD is a large school district located in Johnson County, approximately 115 miles east
of Des Moines. Fiscal 2023 enrollment approximately 14,000 students (basis not stated in the legible text).

## Methodology

**[partial]** US K-12 Public School Districts Methodology, published January 2021.

## Why this document matters

1. **It resolves the rating history.** Combined with `Moodys-ICCSD-2021-03-23-downgrade-Aa1-to-Aa3.pdf` and the
   ratings stated on the GO official statements, the documented chain is:

   | Date | Rating | Source |
   |---|---|---|
   | 29 Nov 2017 | **Aaa** | Series 2017C Official Statement |
   | 6 Dec 2018 | **Aaa** | Series 2019 Official Statement |
   | 20 Jan 2020 | **Aa1** | Series 2020 Official Statement |
   | 23 Mar 2021 | **Aa3** (downgraded from Aa1) | Moody's action, in this folder |
   | 14 Jun 2023 | **Aa3, negative outlook** | this document |
   | 27 Jun 2024 | review opened, direction uncertain | Moody's action of 9 Oct 2024 |
   | 9 Oct 2024 | **Withdrawn** (prior rating Aa3) | `Moodys-ICCSD-2024-10-09-rating-withdrawn.pdf` + List of Affected Credit Ratings |

   This corrects `data/confirmed-rating-actions.csv`, which recorded the prior rating at withdrawal as Aa2.
   Moody's own List of Affected Credit Ratings shows **Aa3** for the issuer rating and all four GO series.
   Note the withdrawal was a batch action covering **61 local-government issuers** (~$3.7B of debt), not an
   ICCSD-specific action — though the stated reason, *"insufficient or otherwise inadequate information to
   support the maintenance of the rating,"* is the audits.

2. **Moody's assigned an outlook it would not normally assign.** The 23 March 2021 action states: *"Moody's does
   not usually assign outlooks to local governments with this amount of debt."* Assigning a negative outlook in
   June 2023 was therefore a deliberate signal, not routine surveillance — roughly **16 months before** the
   October 2024 withdrawal.

3. **It names the cause in advance.** The outlook rationale identifies declining fund balance and cash — the
   exact deterioration the FY24 audit and the 2026 liquidity crisis later confirmed.

4. **It cites "growing enrollment" as a credit strength.** That premise has since reversed: every current
   forecast projects decline to the mid-13,000s by 2030 (see `iccsd-enrollment-forecast.html`).
