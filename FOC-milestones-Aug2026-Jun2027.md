# ICCSD financial milestones — August 2026 through June 2027

*Prepared for the Financial Oversight Committee · unofficial community document · August 2026*

## The through-line

Everything in this period is the road back to **current audits**. FY2024 landed in June 2026, roughly
14 months past its deadline. FY2025 is due around November 2026. **FY2026 is due March 31, 2027.**

That last date is the one to hold onto. Being current on audits is the precondition the district's
financial advisor names for *both* applying for a credit rating and undertaking any capital borrowing.
It is also what the banks said was missing when they declined the district's $25M request in April 2026.

**Key to labels:** **[S]** statutory deadline · **[B]** board action required · **[P]** projection, not a
commitment · **[C]** conditional, depends on a decision not yet made

---

## The three that matter most

1. **September 11, 2026** — closing the $8M draw-down warrant, immediately before the September 15 payroll.
   If it does not close, PFM says an interfund loan will be needed to make that payroll.
2. **September 2026** — $10.6M owed back to the self-insurance fund *and* $7.76M owed in from the SAVE fund,
   in the same month, at the tightest point of the year.
3. **November 2026** — the FY2025 audit. The first real test of whether FY2024's five material weaknesses
   were a one-time cleanup or a pattern.

## Running throughout

- **Monthly financial reports** to the board, the auditor **and the SBRC** — required by SBRC vote on
  June 9, 2026. Whether these are actually going out every month is worth confirming early.
- **FY2023 restatement** in progress.
- **Forensic audit** committed by the board, to begin after the FY2024 and FY2025 audits are complete.
- **State Auditor reaudit** requested under Iowa Code §11.6(4).

---

## What "getting there" unlocks

If the FY2026 audit is filed on time in March 2027, the district is current on its audits for the first
time in years. On the financial advisor's analysis that opens two doors which are currently closed:

- **A credit rating application.** Moody's withdrew the district's Aa3 rating on October 9, 2024 for
  *"insufficient or otherwise inadequate information"* — which for a school district means the audits.
- **Capital borrowing**, which PFM puts no earlier than **spring 2028**, gating roughly $18.5M of planned
  PPEL work.
