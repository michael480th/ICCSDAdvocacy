Six facts

  • Last of 15. Iowa City ranks 15th of the 15 large Iowa districts studied on overall financial health & management — and it isn't close.
  • Audits chronically late. Iowa City filed its FY2024 audit about two years late (June 2026, with five material weaknesses) and its FY2025 audit is still unfiled — the furthest behind of the 15 large districts.
  • Spent past the limit. Its spending authority went negative in 2023 — the only district in the group, and the level that by law triggers a state-supervised recovery.
  • Lost its rating. Lost its bond rating in 2024 and remains unrated — rare for a district this size, where most regain it within months.
  • ~9 days of cushion. About 30 days of operating reserves on hand. The recommended cushion is ~60.
  • Not enrollment. Enrollment is essentially flat (+0.1%/yr) — this is not a shrinking-district problem. It's about how the money was managed.
