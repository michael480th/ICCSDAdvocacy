What's happening

Iowa City's cash cushion shrank from about 53 days in 2020 to just 33 days in 2023 — while comparable districts kept roughly 98 days and the recommended level is 60+. A cushion this thin means a single bad month, a late state payment, or a surprise repair can become a real cash crunch.
