What's happening

Iowa City's cash cushion fell from about 53 days in 2020 to ~33 in 2023. The state-filed 2024 number looked like a rebound — but it didn't stick: 2025 is back to ~33 days, the same low, with 2026 projected around 35. The district has sat near a month of cash for three straight years, while peers held ~98. A cushion this thin means a bad month or a late state payment can become a real cash crunch.
