# Iowa City Community School District CFO History Since 2010

This document summarizes the current and prior finance chief / Chief Financial Officer function for the Iowa City Community School District (ICCSD) since 2010.

The district has not always used the title **Chief Financial Officer** consistently. Earlier records use titles such as **Executive Director of Administrative Services**, **Executive Director of Business Resources and Organizational Effectiveness**, and **Director of Budget & Finance, Treasurer**. Where the exact CFO title was not used, the table identifies the person who appears to have held the top district finance function.

## Key Finding

The biggest uncertainty is the transition from **Craig Hansel** to **Leslie Finger**.

Craig Hansel was definitely CFO through **at least February 2019**, and was publicly described as ICCSD's CFO in a 2019 program. Leslie Finger was definitely the top finance official by the **FY2020 audit**, but that audit uses **Director of Budget & Finance, Treasurer**, not CFO. Finger is explicitly titled **Chief Financial Officer, Treasurer** by the FY2021 audit.

The most defensible wording is:

> **Craig Hansel served July 1, 2012 through sometime in 2019. Leslie Finger became the lead finance officer by FY2020 and was formally titled CFO by FY2021, serving until June 30, 2023.**

## Open Items / Remaining Uncertainty

I did not find a public board action, personnel agenda item, or district announcement that cleanly states either of the following:

1. **Craig Hansel left ICCSD on a specific date.**
2. **Leslie Finger was formally appointed CFO on a specific date.**

For a legally precise or publication-grade timeline, the next step would be to inspect ICCSD board personnel agendas and minutes from approximately **March 2019 through June 2021**, focusing on Hansel's departure, Finger's title change, and any reorganization of the Business Office.
