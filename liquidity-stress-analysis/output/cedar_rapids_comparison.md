# Cedar Rapids comparison — numerator sensitivity

Same five numerator cases as ICCSD, to test whether ICCSD's apparent weakness is definition-specific or persists under broader operating-reserve definitions.

| Numerator case | ICCSD FY2023 days | Cedar Rapids FY2023 days |
|---|---:|---:|
| GF unassigned only | 9 | 61 |
| GF assigned + unassigned | 9 | 61 |
| GF total fund balance | 18 | 68 |
| GF assigned + unassigned + Management | 11 | 80 |
| GF total + Management | 20 | 87 |

**Finding:** Cedar Rapids is stronger under *every* definition. ICCSD's thinness is **not** an artifact of the strict unassigned-only metric — it persists from the conservative case all the way through the broadest (GF total + Management) case. Cedar Rapids' FY2025 composite class: Low risk.
