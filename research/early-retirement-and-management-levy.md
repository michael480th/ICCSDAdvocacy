# Early retirement program and the Management Levy Fund (FY2016–FY2024)

Backup for the narrative beat in Section 7. All figures traced to the Iowa City CSD audited ACFRs,
Note 5 (Long-Term Liabilities) changes schedule, and the FY2024 interfund-balances detail.

## Early-retirement obligations, year by year

The **Additions** column is the new early-retirement incentives the district *granted* that year (the grant
rate). **Reductions** is the cash paid out. Each year's ending balance ties to the next year's beginning,
confirming the series.

| FY | Beginning | New grants (Additions) | Paid out (Reductions) | Ending |
|----|----------:|-----------------------:|----------------------:|-------:|
| 2016 | 1,367,353 | 2,072,779 | 1,318,598 | 2,121,534 |
| 2017 | 2,121,534 | 1,428,917 | 2,110,586 | 1,439,865 |
| 2018 | 1,439,865 | 2,366,466 | 1,439,865 | 2,366,466 |
| **2019** | 2,366,466 | **5,962,182** | 2,366,466 | 5,962,182 |
| 2020 | 5,962,182 | 827,808 | 5,850,489 | 939,501 |
| 2021 | 939,501 | 2,375,381 | 939,501 | 2,375,381 |
| 2022 | 2,375,381 | 1,205,391 | 2,375,381 | 1,205,391 |
| **2023** | 1,205,391 | **7,209,964** | 1,205,391 | 7,209,964 |
| 2024 | 8,080,288* | 3,431,374 | 8,177,763 | 3,333,899 |

\* FY2024 beginning balance was restated up $870,324 from the FY2023 ending of $7,209,964 (Note 15 corrected a
prior under-accrual of the 20-day sick-day benefit).

**Read of the series.** Baseline grant rate is roughly $1.2M–$2.4M/year. Two deliberate buyout waves stand out:
**FY2019 ≈ $5.96M (about 3x)** and **FY2023 ≈ $7.21M (about 6x the prior year, the largest in the decade).**
The FY2023 wave coincided with the year the books came apart and the CFO retired (June 2023), and it was paid
out in FY2024.

## FY2024 specifics (from Note 5 narrative and the interfund detail)

- Early-retirement benefits **paid from the Management Levy Fund in FY2024: $8,177,763.**
- **54 new employee elections in FY2024**; **58 participants** still owed at June 30, 2024; remaining liability
  **$3,333,899**.
- Program terms: a District contribution to a Special Pay Deferral Plan worth **85%–100% of final-year base
  salary**, plus **up to 20 sick days** paid out, based on application date. Participation criteria are set by
  the Board, "may be adjusted annually to assist management in budgetary restraints," and the Board "reserves
  the right to limit the number of early retirements."

## The Management Levy Fund's interfund position (June 30, 2024)

The fund that finances early retirement was one of the largest borrowers in the shared cash pool.

| Fund | Due from other funds | Due to other funds |
|---|---:|---:|
| General Fund | 29,093,296 | 20,073,735 |
| Capital Projects Fund | 8,492,371 | 1,103,189 |
| **Management Levy Fund** | 12,788 | **11,373,033** |
| Nonmajor governmental funds | - | 1,547,982 |
| Nonmajor enterprise funds | - | 4,068,337 |
| Internal Service Funds | 567,821 | - |
| **Total** | **38,166,276** | **38,166,276** |

The Management Levy Fund owed **$11,373,033** to other funds — nearly a third of the district-wide $38.17M of
interfund loans the FY2024 audit flagged as unauthorized (Finding 2024-008). Its dedicated property-tax levy
could not cover the $8.18M of early-retirement payouts, so it drew the difference from the pooled cash.

## What is NOT supported

- No audit *finding* (2024-001 through 014) addresses early retirement.
- The "$1.26M in early retirements beyond board policy" figure circulated by another tool appears **nowhere** in
  any of the ten ICCSD audits (FY2015–FY2024). Do not use it.
- The audits do not publish a Management-Levy budget-to-actual, so no precise "over the certified budget" dollar
  figure can be stated. The defensible claims are the grant-rate waves, the FY2024 payout and election count,
  the $870K restatement, and the $11.37M interfund position.
