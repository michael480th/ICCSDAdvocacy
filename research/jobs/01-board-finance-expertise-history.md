# Research job 1 — ICCSD board financial expertise, full history

**Objective.** Confirm whether the Iowa City school board has *ever* had a member with a
professional finance/accounting background, going back further than 2013. We have 2013–2026 covered
(no finance professional found); we need the earlier record to state "long-standing gap" with confidence.

**Why it matters.** The narrative will argue the board lacked any independent financial-expertise check
when the finance staff function collapsed. To say that fairly, we need to know it wasn't a recent erosion
from a finance-strong past.

## Questions to answer
1. Board members serving roughly **2000–2013**: names, years served, and occupation/profession.
2. Did **any** of them hold a CPA, accounting degree, banking/CFO role, or comparable professional
   finance credential? Flag each one specifically.
3. Did any board in that period publicly position itself as having financial expertise (e.g., a member
   described in coverage as the board's "finance person")?

## Where to look
- Ballotpedia ICCSD election pages (2007, 2009, 2011, 2013) and individual candidate pages.
- Iowa City Press-Citizen and Cedar Rapids Gazette election archives (these are paywalled to automated
  fetching, so a human with subscription access is ideal).
- Johnson County Auditor historic school-election results.
- ICCSD BoardDocs archive and old district board-member pages (Wayback Machine snapshots).

## Output format
A markdown table: `Name | Years served | Occupation | Finance credential? (Y/N + detail) | Source URL`,
followed by a 3-sentence summary stating whether any finance professional ever served and the best source
for it.
