# Research job 3 — The 2017 $191.5M bond referendum, primary details

**Objective.** Get the hard facts and a little color on the September 2017 bond vote so the "big bet"
section is precise and well-sourced.

**Why it matters.** The bond is the opening move of the whole story. We have the amount ($191.5M) and the
"largest school bond in Iowa history" claim confirmed by the Gazette headline. We need the vote specifics
and the project scope to anchor the section.

## Questions to answer
1. **Exact election date** and the **vote result** (yes/no percentages and the supermajority threshold it
   had to clear, which is 60% in Iowa).
2. The **Facilities Master Plan scope** the bond funded: which schools, new buildings vs. renovations,
   and any signature projects (the bond financed the "remaining projects of the District's facility
   master plan" per the FY24 audit).
3. One or two **named officials or board members** quoted at the time, and the district's stated rationale
   (enrollment growth, equity across buildings, aging facilities).
4. Confirm the **"largest in Iowa history"** framing with a citable line, and note whether any later Iowa
   bond exceeded it (for accuracy if we say "largest ever" vs. "largest at the time").

## Where to look
- The Gazette, "Iowa City School District voters pass $191.5 million school bond Tuesday" (paywalled to
  automation): https://www.thegazette.com/k/iowa-city-school-district-voters-pass-191-5-million-school-bond-tuesday/
- Johnson County Auditor official 2017 election canvass.
- The bond Official Statement / preliminary official statement (EMMA, emma.msrb.org) for project lists.
- Iowa City Press-Citizen 2017 coverage.

## Output format
A short fact block: `election date | yes % | threshold | result`, then a 4-6 bullet project-scope list,
then 1-2 attributed quotes with source URLs, then one line confirming the "largest" claim with its source.
