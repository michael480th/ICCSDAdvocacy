# Research job 4 — Did the district's financial-health reporting stop after FY2019?

**Objective.** Determine whether ICCSD kept producing its internal Annual Financial Health Report (and
regular monthly/board financial reporting) after FY2019, or whether that practice died with the CFO who
built it.

**Why it matters.** This is the spine of the "capacity existed, then collapsed" story. We have the
**FY2019 Annual Financial Health Report**, a rigorous 22-page document prepared by CFO Leslie Finger using
a 10-ratio framework. If that was the last one, the contrast is stark and sourced: a district that graded
its own books in red/yellow/green in 2019 could not produce a balance sheet by 2025.

## Questions to answer
1. Were **Annual Financial Health Reports produced for FY2020, FY2021, FY2022, FY2023**? If yes, who
   authored them and are they public. If no, confirm FY2019 was the last.
2. When did **regular monthly financial reporting to the board** become incomplete or stop? The 2026
   coverage says tracking was unreliable "for nearly three years," which implies roughly FY2023 onward.
   Find the board-meeting record that best dates the breakdown.
3. Did the board ever **formally receive and discuss** a financial-health report or dashboard between 2020
   and 2025? Cite the meeting.

## Where to look
- ICCSD BoardDocs (go.boarddocs.com/ia/iccsd) — search board packets 2019-2025 for "Financial Health
  Report," "financial dashboard," "quarterly financial," "treasurer's report."
- The district website financial-transparency/audit pages.
- The Gazette emails investigations (June 2026) for the timeline of when reporting broke down.

## Output format
A table `Fiscal year | Health report produced? | Author | Public link/source`, plus a 3-4 sentence
timeline of when monthly board financial reporting degraded, with the dating source.
