# Research job 5 — ACT/Tyler building: the conflict-of-interest and the valuation gap

**Objective.** Confirm or refute two claims about the district's 2022 purchase of ACT's Tyler Building:
(a) that the seller's leadership included a former ICCSD board member, and (b) that the $8.7M price was well
above the building's assessed value and a prior owner appraisal.

**Why it matters.** The $8.7M PPEL purchase is confirmed and was publicly contested. If the
conflict-of-interest and the valuation gap check out, this becomes a clean, sourced governance/capital-
allocation beat. If they do not, we drop the conflict line and soften the valuation point. **This is the
most sensitive claim in the project. Do not publish it until sourced to a name and dates.**

## Questions to answer
1. **Who controlled/owned the Tyler Building and ACT at the time of sale (2022)?** Name the ACT CEO and any
   relevant ACT board members. Was **any of them a former ICCSD school board member**? If yes: name, ICCSD
   board years, and the role at ACT. Cite a primary source (ACT leadership page, board bios, news).
2. **Assessed value and appraisal.** Confirm the **Johnson County assessed value** of the Tyler Building/land
   around 2021-2022 (county assessor records). Find ACT's **2018/2019 appraisal** (~$4.555M claimed) if it is
   a public document. State the gap vs. the $8.7M price.
3. **Did the University of Iowa decline the building as a donation**, as the community email claims? Source it
   or mark unverified.
4. **The purchase process.** Was there a public needs assessment before the offer? What did the "questions"
   in the 2022 coverage actually concern? Any board member recusal or conflict disclosure on the record?

## Where to look
- Johnson County Assessor / Recorder (assessed values, deed, sale price).
- The Gazette and Daily Iowan June 2022 ACT-purchase coverage (paywalled to automation).
- ACT corporate leadership history (2022) and any board roster.
- ICCSD board minutes June 2022 (BoardDocs) for the vote, any recusal, and the staff analysis.

## Output format
Two sections. **(A) Conflict of interest:** a clear yes/no with the name, ICCSD board dates, ACT role, and
source — or "not found." **(B) Valuation:** a small table `Measure | $ | Date | Source` (price, assessed
value, prior appraisal) and one sentence on the gap. Flag anything that cannot be sourced.
