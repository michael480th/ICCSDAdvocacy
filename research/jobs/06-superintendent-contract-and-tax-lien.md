# Research job 6 — Superintendent contract accountability, and the federal tax lien

**Objective.** Establish, with sources, (a) whether ICCSD's superintendent contract is genuinely unusual in
lacking performance/accountability provisions compared with peer Iowa districts, and (b) the facts of the
$377,628.90 federal tax lien.

**Why it matters.** The defensible version of the contract critique is "no accountability built in, and none
exercised," not "legally unfireable." To say the contract is *unusual*, we need a comparison, not just one
community member's 2022 opinion. The tax lien corroborates the late-filing / IRS-penalty thread.

## Questions to answer — contract
1. What does the **current/most-recent ICCSD superintendent contract** actually contain on **performance
   measures, goals tied to the strategic plan, outside-consulting permission, professional-development caps,
   and termination/buyout terms**? Quote the relevant clauses.
2. Is this **unusual versus peer large Iowa districts** (e.g., Cedar Rapids, Davenport, Des Moines, Waukee,
   Ankeny)? Do those contracts include performance/accountability language ICCSD's lacks? Cite 2-3 comparison
   contracts.
3. What did the **July 2025 renewal** add or change versus the prior contract? Any board discussion of
   accountability at renewal (minutes)?
4. On removal: confirm the board's authority to non-renew (the May window) and that it **chose not to**,
   transitioning Degner to a ~$180K role (4-2). Any buyout/severance figure.

## Questions to answer — tax lien
5. Confirm the **$377,628.90 federal tax lien** (filing date, fiscal year, that it was released, and what it
   was for). Tie it to the reported **$525,110 in IRS penalties (Sept 2023-June 2025)** for late payroll/
   excise filings.

## Where to look
- ICCSD superintendent contracts (BoardDocs; the district posts them as board-action items).
- Peer-district superintendent contracts (their BoardDocs/board packets) for the comparison.
- The Gazette contract coverage (paywalled to automation).
- Johnson County Recorder / federal tax-lien records; the Gazette "former CFO" emails story for the penalty
  figure.

## Output format
**(A) Contract:** quoted clauses on the five items above, plus a short comparison table
`District | Performance language? | Notable terms | Source`. **(B) Tax lien:** one fact block
`amount | filed | released | for what | source`.
