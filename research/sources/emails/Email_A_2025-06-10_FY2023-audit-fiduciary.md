# Question re FY2023 Audit, Exercise of Fiduciary Duty before Voting to approve Field Houses

**From:** [REDACTED SENDER]  
**To:** Board  
**Date:** June 10, 2025 at 10:40 AM

Dear Board Members,

**I appreciate as volunteers that you give your time generously and that it is limited; however, <u>where is ICCSD's FY2023 Audit</u>, which has been pulled from the agenda again?**

To satisfy your **<u>fiduciary duty to the district's children and families, you should read the final FY2023 audit or a draft of it before voting to authorize tens of millions more in spending.</u>** You should also make an inquiry about why ICCSD had a federal tax lien in the amount of $377,628.90 filed against this year (it was released). This lien is a very unusual event.

Under Iowa law, the FY2023 audit was required to be publicly filed with the Iowa Auditor's office last calendar year. It is also required to be filed with the Federal Audit Clearing House. See https://www.fac.gov/

Many districts have already filed their FY 2024 audits because the filing deadline is nine months following the end of the district's fiscal year.

I request that you **<u>not</u>** vote on another facilities master plan until you have reviewed the FY2023 audit and assured yourselves that

- yes, monies will be available for today's and future district's children's needs without requiring a general obligation bond vote or closing neighborhood elementary schools,
- the district will continue to have enough general fund monies to operate its existing buildings and employ staff, including a sufficient number of teachers, and
- there is enough PPEL funding to properly maintain the existing buildings the district has--because if PPEL funds are insufficient, more eligible PPEL expenses will be pushed into the general fund and put undue pressure on the general fund. (I also do not support spending over $2 million dollars on a Dream Accelerator that provides no industry recognized credentials to ICCSD's students and will cost the general fund bussing monies at a minimum.)

Please also consider reviewing the FY2024 audit prior to an FMP vote. It should have been filed by the end of March of this year.

Further, before you vote on field houses (not an academic need), fiduciary duty would require due diligence (investigation, ask questions verify information, make a fully informed decision)--what is in them, what is the net cost to the general fund to operate them, will maintaining them and what's inside them put pressure on the PPEL fund, which will result in more expenses being pushed into the general fund, what "outside organizations" were involved in working on a master plan and did they have any conflicts of interest? ICCSD's middle school additions were clearly over budget. As a parent, and perhaps future grandparent, athletic facilities, while important, should not trump academic needs (even more important) like paying teachers (including experienced teachers) and operating elementary and secondary schools.

Building because "students want us to build things" does not satisfy the fiduciary requirement of due diligence and is not representative of Iowa's financial literacy standards for K12.

Thank you for your service.
