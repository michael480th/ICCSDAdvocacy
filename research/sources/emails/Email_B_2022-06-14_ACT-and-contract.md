# Data Driven District - Objections to Board signing Superintendent Contract and Offer to Purchase ACT's Tyler building

**From:** [REDACTED SENDER]  
**To:** Board & 2 more  
**Date:** June 14, 2022 at 11:52 AM

Dear Board Members,

If this district and board are to be **data driven and results oriented, I am writing to request that you not sign the superintendent's contract and the Offer to Purchase ACT's Tyler Building.** Please note this email is not a comment about the superintendent's performance.

- The Tyler building offer deserves your highest scrutiny and due diligence, especially since the CEO of its owner is a former ICCSD board member.
- Performance measures and accountability should be built into the superintendent contract. They are not.

1) I would have expected that prior to any offer to purchase a building using public dollars there first be a discussion of need. That discussion has not taken place in public.

   - The Tyler building has an above grade square footage of 83,128 SF and below grade square footage of 41,446 SF for a total building area of 124,574 SF.
   - The land area is 344,124 SF or 7.9 acres.
   - To put that square footage in perspective, a football field is 57,600 square feet and an acre is 43,560 square feet.

**What do you propose to do with the space that you require so much of it? What other alternatives were considered and rejected?**

2) As part of this email, **I request that you send me the analysis the administration provided the board to show the projected remaining economic life span of the Tyler building, the yearly operational costs (including any road costs), and the costs to renovate it for your intended use.**

   - What is the source of funding for the purchase, renovation, and operating expenses?
   - What will your ownership of this building cost the general fund?
   - Once COVID relief money runs out, are not you concerned about pressure on the general fund?

3) **It appears the district is overpaying for this forty-year old building.**

   - ACT has been appealing its assessed valuations.
   - The current assessed value is $5,365,000.
   - ACT's own 2018/2019 appraisal (public document) **valued the Tyler Building and land at $4,555,000.**
   - Isn't this building the one the University of Iowa rejected as a donation?
   - The Tyler building, given its age and location is likely to be difficult to resell or give away.
   - How did you arrive at the dollar figure for your purchase offer?

4) Please do not occupy the Tyler building prior to a public discussion of need, inspections, financial analysis, and reevaluation of the purchase price. This purchase deserves due diligence!

5) **Please do not again sign a superintendent contract without any performance measures and metrics.**

   - A board should have the means to hold its superintendent accountable for delivering results, including positive academic outcomes for all groups.
   - **This superintendent contract provides little to nothing in the way of accountability and looks more favorable to the individual who holds the superintendent position than the children of this district.**
   - The contract does not provide future board members with the ability to manage and hold the board's only employee accountable.

6) The superintendent contract is not aligned to the district's strategic plan, which it should be.

7) What will the superintendent contract cost the district upon termination?

   - I'm assuming the board received an analysis, please send it to me.
   - It cost Des Moines Public Schools a significant amount of money to terminate its relationship with former superintendent, Tom Ahert, that made bad press.
   - Do you know how much vacation time is being carried over?
   - How much did the district pay out to Murley upon termininination?
   - Why isn't professional development capped? My recollection is it used to be.

8) I am disappointed but not surprised that the contract continues to permit the superintendent to consult outside the district. Being a superintendent should be a full time job, but if permitted, all consulting should come before the **entire board** in public.

Thank you for your service.
